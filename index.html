Chill Temperature & Hobart Mixer Friction Control

Commercial mixers like the Hobart 20-Quart generate kinetic friction during high-speed masa aeration. Uncontrolled heat melts fat particles prematurely, causing heavy, dense tamales.

1

Cold Lard Whipping

Ensure lard/fat is chilled to 40°F (4°C) before whipping in the Hobart bowl. Cold fat creates micro-air pockets critical for ultimate fluffiness.

2

Ice-Cold Broth/Water

Always incorporate ice-cold chicken broth or iced spiced liquid when hydrating the masa. Never add warm broth directly to whipped fat.

3

Friction Control Bursts

Commercial mixing generates rapid heat build-up. Cool the Hobart bowl jacket or mix in 3-minute bursts to prevent fat breakdown.
